One reference with why it fits, the record fields that support it, two status badges and the actions.

## Use
- Show the artwork whole on a mat. Never crop or filter it.
- "Why it fits" is written from the record only. Evidence chips name the record fields (for example `period`, `medium`) and link to the museum record.
- The badges sit together under the rationale. A card with "Permission required" shows metadata and a link instead of the image.
- Actions: "More like this" (anchor), "Report" (this is wrong), "Keep" checkbox (selection for approval). Keep is a real control with a visible focus ring.
- A selected card uses `accent` for its border, not a coloured side stripe.

## Tokens
`card`, `sunken`, `line`, `line-strong`, `accent`, `accent-soft`, `radius-lg`, `shadow-card`, display and mono type styles.

## The consumer provides
The normalised record (title, artist, date, source, image URL, rights record), the rationale, the evidence fields and the verification result.
